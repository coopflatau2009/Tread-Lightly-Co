Our Story

Tread Lightly Co. was founded by me, Cooper Flatau, a 16-year-old from Minnesota with a love for nature, hunting, and the great outdoors.

There is nothing quite like waiting all year for opener to finally arrive. That anticipation, the time spent outside, and the memories made in the outdoors are what inspired Tread Lightly Co.

My goal is to share that passion with generations to come. I want to do that through apparel and accessories, original artwork, collaborations with others, and by supporting the conservation of the places and traditions that make the outdoors worth protecting.

This is just the beginning. Tread Lightly Co. is about sharing the passion, respecting the outdoors, and keeping it going for many generations to come.
