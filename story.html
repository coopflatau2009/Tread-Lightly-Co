Our Story

Tread Lightly Co. was founded by Cooper Flatau, a young man born and raised in a small town in Minnesota with a passion for hunting and spending time in the great outdoors.

There is nothing quite like waiting all year for opener to finally arrive. That anticipation, the time spent preparing, and the memories made in the process of it all are what inspired Tread Lightly Co.

My goal is to share that passion with generations to come. Bringing the passion to life through original artwork, apparel, accessories, posters, and more.

-Cooper Flatau, founder of Tread Lightly CO.
